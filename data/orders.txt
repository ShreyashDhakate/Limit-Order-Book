# Sample order flow. Format:
#   NEW <id> <trader> <BUY|SELL> <price> <qty> [GTC|IOC|FOK|MKT]
#   MOD <id> <new_price> <new_qty>   |   CXL <id>
#   PRINT | BBO | DEPTH <n>

# --- Build a resting book (no crosses yet) ---------------------------------
NEW 1 100 BUY  99  50
NEW 2 100 BUY  98  30
NEW 3 101 BUY  99  20          # same price as #1 -> sits behind it (time priority)
NEW 4 200 SELL 101 40
NEW 5 200 SELL 102 60
PRINT
BBO

# --- A buy that crosses the spread and partially fills ---------------------
NEW 6 300 BUY 101 25           # lifts 25 of the 40 resting at 101
DEPTH 3

# --- Immediate-or-cancel: takes what it can, discards the rest -------------
NEW 7 300 BUY 101 100 IOC      # fills remaining 15 @ 101, drops the other 85

# --- Fill-or-kill that CANNOT fully fill -> does nothing -------------------
NEW 8 400 SELL 99 1000 FOK     # not enough bid depth, so zero trades

# --- Fill-or-kill that CAN fully fill -------------------------------------
NEW 9 400 SELL 99 60 FOK       # 50 @ #1 + 10 @ #3, all-or-nothing satisfied

# --- Market order sweeps the best price, never rests ----------------------
NEW 10 500 SELL 0 10 MKT       # hits the best remaining bid

# --- Cancel + modify --------------------------------------------------------
CXL 2                          # remove the 98 bid
MOD 5 100 60                   # reprice ask #5 from 102 -> 100

PRINT
BBO
